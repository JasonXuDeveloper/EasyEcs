Layers of a tile padded with their halo ([`Tile.Load`](/en/docs/reference/tile#load-2)): the texture a stencil samples. Walk the rows to shade with [`PaddedTile.Rows`](/en/docs/reference/paddedtile#rows) (the active ones, by counting trailing zeros) and sample around a row with [`PaddedTile.At`](/en/docs/reference/paddedtile#at): every sample up to [`ITileShape.Halo`](/en/docs/reference/itileshape#halo) cells, rows or layers away stays inside the texture, which is checked when the offsets are not constants and folded away when they are. Valid until the thread loads this tile type again.

#### Type parameters

- `TCell`: The cell.
- `TTile`: The tile's component and shape.

The texture at the first cell of `row` (a row of layers From to To, from [`PaddedTile.Rows`](/en/docs/reference/paddedtile#rows) of this tile or of another loaded with the same layers): its samples. Throws for a row outside. O(1).

### `Rows` [#rows]

```csharp
public TileRows<TTile> Rows(TileReads reads = All);
```

The rows of layers From to To that a stencil reading `reads` must shade, layer by layer, in order (`foreach (var row in padded.Rows(TileReads.Layer))`): those with a cell that is not zero among the rows it reads, up to the halo away. O(1) per row visited and per layer. Throws when the tile was loaded without its mask.

**Complexity** ([API table](/en/docs/api)): O(1) per layer and per row visited: a trailing-zero count
