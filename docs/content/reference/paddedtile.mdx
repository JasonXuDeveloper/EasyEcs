Layers of a tile padded with their halo ([`Tile.Load`](/en/docs/reference/tile#load-2)): the texture a stencil samples. Walk the rows to shade with [`PaddedTile.Rows`](/en/docs/reference/paddedtile#rows) (the active ones, by counting trailing zeros) and sample around a row with [`PaddedTile.At`](/en/docs/reference/paddedtile#at): every sample up to [`ITileShape.Halo`](/en/docs/reference/itileshape#halo) cells, rows or layers away stays inside the texture, which is checked when the offsets are not constants and folded away when they are. Valid until the thread loads this tile type again.

#### Type parameters

- `TCell`: The cell.
- `TTile`: The tile's component and shape.

### `Steps` [#steps]

```csharp
public PaddedTile<TCell, TTile> Steps<TStep, TLane>() where TStep : struct, ITileStep<TCell, TTile, TLane>;
```

[`ITileShape.Halo`](/en/docs/reference/itileshape#halo) / `TStep.Reach` steps of a stencil in one pass (temporal blocking): each step computes the texture with `TStep` into this thread's second texture and the next step reads it, ping-pong, the halo shrinking by the reach each step (the cells further out no longer have what they read), so after the last the tile's own cells hold the stencil applied that many times, from one load. The rows a step skips ([`ITileStep.Reads`](/en/docs/reference/itilestep#reads)) are recomputed every step, and where there is no neighbour the halo keeps following the boundary ([`ITileShape.Boundary`](/en/docs/reference/itileshape#boundary)). Write the result with [`PaddedTile.CopyTo`](/en/docs/reference/paddedtile#copyto). Needs the tile's mask, a neighbourhood of [`TileNeighbourhood.All`](/en/docs/reference/tileneighbourhood#all) for more than one step (the cells a few steps reach lie across corners), Depth + 2r ≤ 64 and rows of at least 16 bytes.

O(steps × layers × (Width + 2r) × (Depth + 2r)) at most, no allocation once the thread has its second texture. The result is valid until the thread loads or steps this tile type again.

**Type parameters**

- `TStep`: The step: a struct, its `Step` compiled into the loop.
- `TLane`: The lanes the step computes in.

**Complexity** ([API table](/en/docs/api)): O(steps × layers × (Width + 2r) × (Depth + 2r)) at most; steps = `Halo / TStep.Reach`
