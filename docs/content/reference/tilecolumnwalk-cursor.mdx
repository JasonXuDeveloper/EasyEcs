Nested in [`TileColumnWalk`](/en/docs/reference/tilecolumnwalk).

The walk at a column of the row: the cells around it, one or a vector at a time, at offsets from the column, and the store of a vector into the row the step writes at the column. An offset is in cells: dx from −r up to where a load from the walk's last column still ends by the row's last halo cell (Width + r − 1), dy and dz from −r to r (dy 0 for a 2D tile), r the shape's [`ITileShape.Halo`](/en/docs/reference/itileshape#halo). Every column of the walk passes the same check, so constant offsets with a constant step are checked by the JIT, others by a compare.
