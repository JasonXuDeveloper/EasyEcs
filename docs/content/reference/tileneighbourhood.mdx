Which tiles around a tile are its neighbours, by how many axes their offset moves along ([`ITileShape.Neighbourhood`](/en/docs/reference/itileshape#neighbourhood)): the tiles across a face (von Neumann), also across an edge, or also across a corner (Moore). In 2D a tile has 4, 8 and 8; in 3D 6, 18 and 26. Only the axes of [`ITileShape.Faces`](/en/docs/reference/itileshape#faces) count: a voxel column with neighbours on its sides only has 4, 8 and 8.

## Fields

### `All` [#all]


Every tile around (offsets along up to three axes): a 9-point 2D or 27-point 3D stencil.

### `Faces` [#faces]

```csharp
Faces = 0;
```

The tiles across a face (offsets along one axis): a five- or seven-point stencil reads no more.

### `FacesAndEdges` [#facesandedges]

```csharp
FacesAndEdges = 1;
```

Also the tiles across an edge (offsets along two axes): the corners of a 2D grid, a 19-point stencil.
