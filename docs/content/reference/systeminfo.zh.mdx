#### 参数 [#parameters]

- `Name`：The system type's name.
- `Reads`：Components its [ForEach] methods read (`in` parameters, filters of [Changed&lt;T&gt;]).
- `Writes`：Components its [ForEach] methods write (`ref` parameters).
- `ReadsSingletons`：Singletons it reads.
- `WritesSingletons`：Singletons it writes.
- `RunsAlone`：It may touch anything (an OnUpdate method, an Entity or World parameter...): it never shares a stage.
- `After`：Why it is not in an earlier stage: the systems of the stage just before its own that it must follow (empty in the first stage).
