```csharp
public ref struct Tile<TCell, TTile> where TCell : unmanaged where TTile : struct, ITileShape
```

The cells of a tile (an entity whose `TTile` makes it one, [`ITile`](/en/docs/reference/itile)) with those of its neighbors: a `[ForEach]` parameter, as a CUDA block's tile and its apron, or an MPI rank's subdomain and its ghost cells. The generated job finds the neighbors by key (one search per neighbor and entity for every tile of the grid), and [`Tile.ExchangeHalo`](/en/docs/reference/tile#exchangehalo-2) copies the layers the stencil shades into the tile's padded texture: the entity's cells in the middle, a halo of [`ITileShape.Halo`](/en/docs/reference/itileshape#halo) cells around them from the neighbors' facing cells, and the boundary ([`ITileShape.Boundary`](/en/docs/reference/itileshape#boundary)) where there is no neighbor, so the stencil has no edge cases. [`Tile.Rows`](/en/docs/reference/tile#rows) walks the rows to shade, [`Tile.At`](/en/docs/reference/tile#at) samples around one.

Taken by value (`Tile<Heat, Plate> heat`) it reads: the system is scheduled as a reader of `DynamicBuffer<TCell>` and of the grid's component (and builds its index before the pass), so every neighbor is read as it was before the pass, and the method may run in parallel. Taken by `ref` (`static void Diffuse(ref Tile<Heat, Plate> heat)`) the method steps the tile: it writes the step's rows through [`Tile.Write`](/en/docs/reference/tile#write), into a second buffer the generated pass keeps beside the cells and swaps with them once every tile stepped (Jacobi, MPI's u / v swap: the rows it skips are written as zero), or in place, color by color, sweeping until the tile settles ([[GaussSeidel]](/en/docs/reference/gaussseidelattribute)). The pass finds the faces whose edge changed from the rows written, and steps only the tiles that may change: those whose cells or whose neighbors' facing edges changed since they last stepped.

Valid for the call it is passed to (a ref struct).

#### Type parameters

- `TCell`: The cell: the element of the entity's `DynamicBuffer<TCell>`.
- `TTile`: The tile's shape: the component that places the entity in the grid, or [`Columns`](/en/docs/reference/columns) of it.

### `ExchangeHalo` [#exchangehalo-2]

```csharp
public void ExchangeHalo(int from, int to, TCell border = default, bool mask = true);
```

Layers `from` to `to` copied with their halo into this thread's texture of the tile, which [`Tile.Rows`](/en/docs/reference/tile#rows), [`Tile.At`](/en/docs/reference/tile#at) and the indexer read: each layer's cells in the middle, a ring of [`ITileShape.Halo`](/en/docs/reference/itileshape#halo) (r) cells around each layer (the facing columns of the west and east neighbors, the facing rows of the north and south ones, the corners from the tiles across the edges when the neighborhood has them), and, for a tile of more than one layer, r layers under `from` and over `to` (the tile's own, or the facing layers of the neighbors below or above). Where there is no neighbor (none in the grid, an empty buffer, or outside the shape's faces and neighborhood) the halo follows [`ITileShape.Boundary`](/en/docs/reference/itileshape#boundary): `border`, or the tile's own edge clamped or mirrored (along the axis that has no neighbor; a tile across an edge or corner whose axis is clamped is read from the tile beside). With `mask`, it records which rows hold a cell that is not zero (every bit), for [`Tile.Rows`](/en/docs/reference/tile#rows). In a [[GaussSeidel]](/en/docs/reference/gaussseidelattribute) step, the sweeps after the first keep the texture they wrote (the neighbors, of other colors, do not change meanwhile): the call copies nothing.

O(layers × (Width + 2r) × (Depth + 2r)), no allocation once the thread has its texture (allocated on its first exchange, for all the layers). The texture belongs to the thread and to this tile type: the next exchange of `TCell` and `TTile` on the thread replaces it, so a method takes one tile per cell type and grid (EECS073). Throws when the shape is invalid (EECS072), the entity's buffer or a neighbor's that is not empty holds fewer cells than the shape, or the layers are not 0 ≤ from ≤ to &lt; Height.

**Complexity** ([API table](/en/docs/api)):

- `tile.ExchangeHalo(from, to, fill, mask)`: O(layers × (Width + 2r) × (Depth + 2r)), r = `Halo`; each row one or two vector copies
- `tile.ExchangeHalo()`: O(layers × (Width + 2r) × (Depth + 2r)), r = `Halo`; each row one or two vector copies

### `Write` [#write]

```csharp
public readonly Span<TCell> Write(TileRow<TTile> row);
```

The cells of `row` in the step, Width of them, for a stepping tile (a `ref Tile` parameter): the row of the second buffer, which becomes the cells once every tile stepped (Jacobi), or, in a [[GaussSeidel]](/en/docs/reference/gaussseidelattribute) step, the row of the texture itself (a row of the layers exchanged), which the next rows and sweeps read. Write every cell of the row: the rows the step does not write are zero after it. O(1); throws for a tile taken by value.
