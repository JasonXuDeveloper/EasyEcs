The cells of a tile (an entity whose `TTile` makes it one, [`ITile`](/en/docs/reference/itile)) with those of its neighbours: a `[ForEach]` parameter, as a CUDA block's tile and halo. `static void Diffuse(Tile<Heat,
Plate> heat, Mut<DynamicBuffer<NextHeat>> next)`: the generated job finds the neighbours by key (one search per entity for every tile of the grid), and [`Tile.Load`](/en/docs/reference/tile#load-2) copies the layers the stencil shades into a padded tile ([`PaddedTile`](/en/docs/reference/paddedtile)): the entity's cells in the middle, a halo of one cell around them from the neighbours' facing rows, columns or layers, and the fill value where there is no neighbour, so the stencil has no edge cases.

The contract: the system is scheduled as a reader of `DynamicBuffer<TCell>` and of `TTile` (and builds its index before the pass), so every neighbour is read as it was before the pass, and the method may run in parallel; a method that writes either of them may not take the tile (EECS059). Write the step into another component and commit it in another system (double buffering).

Valid for the call it is passed to (a ref struct).

#### Type parameters

- `TCell`: The cell: the element of the entity's `DynamicBuffer<TCell>`.
- `TTile`: The component that places the entity in the grid, with the tile's shape.

### `Load` [#load-2]

```csharp
public PaddedTile<TCell, TTile> Load(int from, int to, TCell fill = default, bool mask = true);
```

Layers `from` to `to` padded into this thread's texture: each layer's cells in the middle, a ring of one cell around each layer (the facing column of the west and east neighbours, the facing row of the north and south ones, `fill` at the four corners), and, for a tile of more than one layer, a layer under `from` and over `to` (the tile's own, or the facing layer of the neighbour below or above, its ring `fill`). A face with no neighbour, or whose neighbour's buffer is empty, is `fill`. With `mask`, it records which rows hold a cell that is not zero (every bit), for [`PaddedTile.Rows`](/en/docs/reference/paddedtile#rows).

O(layers × (Width + 2) × (Depth + 2)), no allocation once the thread has its texture (allocated on its first load, for all the layers). The texture belongs to the thread and to this tile type: the next load of `TCell` and `TTile` on the thread replaces it, so a method takes one tile per cell type and grid. Throws when the entity's buffer, or a neighbour's that is not empty, holds fewer cells than the shape, or the layers are not 0 ≤ from ≤ to &lt; Height.

**Complexity** ([API table](/en/docs/api)):

- `tile.Load(from, to, fill, mask)`: O(layers × (Width + 2) × (Depth + 2)), each row one or two vector copies
- `tile.Load()`: O(layers × (Width + 2) × (Depth + 2)), each row one or two vector copies
