A system added to a world ([`World.AddSystem`](/zh/docs/reference/world#addsystem), [`World.System`](/zh/docs/reference/world#system), [`World.Systems`](/zh/docs/reference/world#systems)): a small handle over the world's record of it, as an [`Entity`](/zh/docs/reference/entity) is over its row. The record is data the world owns (enabled, error, ticks, counts, stats); no system object exists. Once the system is removed (or the world disposed) the handle is stale: [`SystemHandle.IsAlive`](/zh/docs/reference/systemhandle#isalive) is false and its other members throw `InvalidOperationException`, even after the world reuses the record for another system. Hooks (`OnUpdate` and the others) may take it to read their own system's record.
