The columns of a tile: a tile of one layer, Width × Depth, on the grid of `TTile` (a tile of several layers), with its sides as faces, its halo, neighborhood and boundary. A second field on the same entities and keys: `Tile<Scent, Columns<Chunk>>` is a chunk's ground beside the `Tile<Water, Chunk>` of its blocks, its cells a `DynamicBuffer<Scent>` of Width × Depth.

#### Type parameters

- `TTile`: The grid's tile component.

The width of the halo, in cells (default 1): the ghost cells of an MPI halo exchange, the apron of a CUDA tile. A stencil reaching r cells, rows or layers away needs r. At most the tile's extent along every axis with neighbors (and along every axis for [`TileBoundary.Mirror`](/en/docs/reference/tileboundary#mirror)): a halo never reaches past the tile beside.
