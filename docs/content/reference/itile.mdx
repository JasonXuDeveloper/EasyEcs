public interface ITile<TKey> : ITileShape, IIndexed<TKey> where TKey : IEquatable<TKey>

A component that makes its entity a tile of a grid, found by its key (an indexed component, [`IIndexed`](/en/docs/reference/iindexed)): `record struct Chunk(ChunkPos Pos) : IComponent, ITile<ChunkPos>` with its shape ([`ITileShape`](/en/docs/reference/itileshape)) and the key of each neighbor ([`ITile.Beside`](/en/docs/reference/itile#beside)). A `[ForEach]` method taking a [`Tile`](/en/docs/reference/tile) of it gets the entity's cells with those of its neighbors, found through the index.

#### Type parameters

- `TKey`: The key: the tile's position in the grid.

## Methods

### `Beside` [#beside]

```csharp
static abstract TKey Beside(TKey key, TileFaces face);
```

The key of the tile beside the one at `key` across `face` (one face of [`ITileShape.Faces`](/en/docs/reference/itileshape#faces)). The tiles across an edge or a corner are found by stepping across one face at a time, x first, then z, then y. On a periodic grid it wraps (the last tile's east is the first).
