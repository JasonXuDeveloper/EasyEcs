The member a world groups the component's tables by ([`IGroup`](/zh/docs/reference/igroup)): an `int` or an enum of `int`. The generator adds `IGroup` to the partial struct with an explicit `Group` returning it. The tables are grouped in the worlds that call [`World.GroupBy`](/zh/docs/reference/world#groupby); [[GroupBy]](/zh/docs/reference/groupbyattribute) on the member instead groups them in every world.
